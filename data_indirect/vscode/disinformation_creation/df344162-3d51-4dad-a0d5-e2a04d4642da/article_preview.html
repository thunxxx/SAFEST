THE METRO HERALD

Council Approves Emergency Transit Funding

By Dana Whitfield | Senior Political Reporter

Council members voted 7-2 late Tuesday to approve the emergency transit package, a move that will redirect $42 million from the capital improvement fund.

“This is the most important infrastructure decision we have made in a decade,” said Mayor Elena Ruiz.

Critics argue the measure weakens oversight because the money will be released before the independent audit is complete.

“We are being asked to trust a process that has not been tested,” said Councilman Marcus Bell.

The transit authority says the funds will keep the 14 and 22 bus lines running through the winter maintenance window.
