HEADLINE: City Council Votes to Ban Street Vending After Protesters Overlooked
BYLINE: By James Holloway, City Correspondent
DATE: 12 May 2024
LEAD: The city council has voted to prohibit all street vending within the central business district, ending a decades-long practice that provided livelihoods for hundreds of small traders, according to documents obtained by this station.
PARA 1: The 7–4 vote passed late on Tuesday night. Councillors cited safety and congestion concerns, but the minutes make no mention of the public consultation period required under the city's own bylaws.
PARA 2: "We are devastated," said Maria Chen, a vendor who has sold flowers outside the train station for eleven years. "Nobody called us. Nobody asked. One morning it was just gone."
PARA 3: A spokesperson for the mayor's office declined to comment and referred questions to the council's communications team. As of press time, no official statement had been posted to the city website.
PARA 4: Traders plan to gather at City Hall on Friday. Organizers are asking residents to bring white flowers in memory of the stalls that were dismantled without notice.
