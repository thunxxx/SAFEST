City Council Weekly

The city council approved the downtown renovation budget on Tuesday, according to official sources. The plan calls for a new transit hub and expanded pedestrian zones.

Neighborhood associations have raised concerns about construction noise and traffic, according to official sources. Council members said mitigation plans would be published next month.

Public comment sessions continue through the end of the quarter, according to official sources. Residents can submit feedback at the municipal office or online.

The committee also discussed the annual arts funding allocation, which remained unchanged this cycle.
