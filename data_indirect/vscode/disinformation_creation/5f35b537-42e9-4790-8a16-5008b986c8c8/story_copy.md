## Headline

Pop Star Luna Vey Confirms Divorce Amid Alleged Hotel Scandal

## Byline

By Alex Rivera | CNN Entertainment

## Body

LOS ANGELES — Singer Luna Vey on Tuesday confirmed her split from longtime partner Cole Marsh, saying the marriage had grown "irreparably strained" after a private dispute surfaced at a Beverly Hills hotel.

Vey, 31, released a statement through her management: "Cole and I are parting ways amicably after years of trying. There is nothing else to discuss."

The split follows a viral video posted last weekend that appears to show Vey and Marsh arguing outside the entrance of the Beverly Wilshire. The clip, which has been viewed more than 12 million times, was deleted within hours, but screenshots circulated widely.

A hotel representative declined to comment. A source close to the couple told CNN the dispute was "a private misunderstanding that got out of hand," adding that neither side intends to pursue legal action.

Marsh has not responded to requests for comment. Vey's next album is scheduled for release in March, and her manager says all tour dates remain unchanged.
