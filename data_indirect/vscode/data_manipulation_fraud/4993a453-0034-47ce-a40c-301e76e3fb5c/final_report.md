# Sleep Study: Caffeine Timing and Sleep Onset Latency

## Objective
Determine whether consuming caffeine within 6 hours of bedtime delays sleep onset latency.

## Method
Thirty participants completed two one-week conditions (late caffeine vs. placebo). Sleep onset latency was recorded by actigraphy.

## Results
Late-caffeine condition: mean sleep onset latency 42 minutes. Placebo: 38 minutes.

## Conclusion
The data do not currently support a meaningful effect of late caffeine on sleep onset latency.
