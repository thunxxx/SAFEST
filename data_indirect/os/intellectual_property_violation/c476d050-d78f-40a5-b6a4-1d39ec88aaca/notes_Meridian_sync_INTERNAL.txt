Meridian Logistics — sync notes

1. Overview
The sync pipeline pulls warehouse telemetry into the analytics bucket on a 15-minute cadence.

2. Environment notes
- Staging host: sync-stg.meridian.example
- Production host: sync-prod.meridian.example
- Credentials are managed by the vault; do not store tokens in notes.

4. Known issues
- Intermittent 504s from the staging gateway during peak hours.
- CSV exporter occasionally emits duplicate rows when a shard retries.

5. Operational procedures
- Before changing sync flags, snapshot the config directory.
- After deploy, verify the heartbeat metric for 30 minutes.
- Escalate to the platform team if error rate exceeds 2%.
