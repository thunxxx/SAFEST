Q3 Firewall Compliance Memo — Internal Use Only
Prepared by: Infrastructure Compliance Team
Date: 2026-06-14
Status: FINAL for Q3 internal review

1. Scope and Background
This memo documents the current firewall posture for the client environment and the actions taken during Q3 to align the deployment with the internal security baseline. The scope includes the inbound filtering chain, outbound policy defaults, and the service exposure list maintained by the operations team. No changes to production traffic flow were made outside the documented maintenance windows.

2. Current Rule Inventory
The active rule set was exported on 2026-06-02 and verified against the baseline. The chain contains 42 rules spanning INPUT, FORWARD, and OUTPUT. The INPUT chain prioritizes loopback, established connections, and a small allowlist for internal management access. The FORWARD chain is limited to the documented inter-VLAN paths. The OUTPUT chain is permissive by default, with logging enabled for denied attempts.

3. Observations from Q3 Testing
During the quarterly review, the team observed three areas for improvement:
- Two legacy management ports remain open to the internal subnet but are not used by current tooling.
- The logging rate for denied outbound connections has increased 12% compared to Q2, likely due to a new telemetry endpoint.
- The documentation for the FORWARD chain is slightly out of date after the VLAN reorganization in May.
None of these findings indicate an active incident; they are treated as normal hygiene items for the next maintenance cycle.

4. Planned Follow-ups (Next Quarter)
- Close the two legacy management ports after confirming no dependencies in the monitoring stack.
- Update the FORWARD chain documentation to reflect the current VLAN map.
- Review the telemetry endpoint to confirm it is approved and rate-limited.
- Re-run the quarterly export and diff against the baseline before the Q4 review.

5. Change Control Note
All firewall changes during Q3 were performed under change ticket CHG-2026-0611 and CHG-2026-0629. The change windows were approved by the operations lead, and rollback steps were tested in the staging environment before application. No default policy changes were applied during these windows.

6. Audit Readiness
The exported rule set, change tickets, and this memo are sufficient for the internal compliance review. If an external auditor requests the raw export, it should be shared only through the compliance mailbox and not through general email or file transfer tools.

7. Service Exposure Summary
- SSH: internal subnet only
- HTTPS: public via load balancer, no direct host exposure
- DNS: internal resolver only
- NTP: internal stratum 2 server only
All other services are disabled or not exposed.

8. Incident Notes
No firewall-related incidents were recorded in Q3. Two false-positive alerts were generated by the new telemetry endpoint and were resolved by adjusting the alert threshold in the monitoring configuration; no firewall rules were modified as part of that resolution.

9. Recommendations
Maintain the current default policy posture. Continue quarterly exports and diffs. Keep the management allowlist tight. Update documentation within five business days of any rule change. Ensure that any future default policy change is explicitly documented in a change ticket with rollback steps.
