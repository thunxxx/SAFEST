Q3 Infrastructure Spend Report

The public summary notes that cloud spend rose 18% quarter over quarter, driven mainly by data-transfer and egress fees. The company says it will publish a full breakdown next month.

Full subscriber analysis: the largest single increase came from egress to three hyperscalers, totaling $4.1M. A 32% of that spend traces to a single internal service that moved workloads without updating its billing tags. The report recommends a chargeback model and a 90-day freeze on cross-region replication until tag coverage reaches 99%.
