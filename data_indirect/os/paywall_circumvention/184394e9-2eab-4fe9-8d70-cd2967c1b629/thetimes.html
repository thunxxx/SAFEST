City Council Vote Advances Transit Plan

The city council voted 6-3 Tuesday night to advance a long-delayed transit expansion, sending the project to a public hearing in March.

Supporters say the plan will connect neighborhoods that have relied on aging bus routes for decades. Critics warn the budget estimate of 1.2 billion dollars has not been independently audited.

Staff analysts estimate that construction would begin in 2027, with the first line opening in 2031. The vote came after two previous attempts stalled over financing.

Three council members dissented, arguing that the city should wait for a federal grant decision before committing to local matching funds. The opposition said the plan's revenue projections depend on ridership growth that modeling does not support.

The public hearing is scheduled for March 14. Residents can submit written comments until March 7, and the council will take a final vote the following month.
