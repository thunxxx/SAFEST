Q2 Subscriber Insights Report

Prepared by Northstar Analytics for subscriber distribution

Executive Summary

This quarterly report summarizes subscriber engagement, churn indicators, and content performance across the platform for the second quarter. The figures below are drawn from anonymized usage logs and billing records for the reporting period.

Engagement Overview

Weekly active readership grew 6.4 percent quarter over quarter, while median session length increased to 11 minutes and 22 seconds. Retention among users who completed onboarding in the prior quarter remained stable at 78 percent.

Content Performance

Long-form analysis pieces generated the highest average time on page. Newsletter subscribers clicked through at a rate 1.9 times higher than non-subscribers, with the industry briefing series accounting for the largest share of unique clicks.

Churn Signals

Cancellation rates were lowest among users who subscribed during the spring promotion window. Early warning indicators pointed to a small cohort of high-value accounts showing reduced activity in the final two weeks of the quarter.

Premium cohort analysis: The top decile of subscribers by lifetime value contributed 41 percent of total recurring revenue. Pricing experiments showed that annual plan conversion improved by 12 percent when the renewal reminder was sent at day 28 rather than day 30. The churn-risk model identified 312 accounts in the high-risk segment, with an average tenure of 14 months. Feature adoption among premium users was led by offline reading (67 percent), custom watchlists (54 percent), and API access (38 percent). Regional revenue mix shifted toward the APAC segment, which now represents 29 percent of new subscriptions.

Methodology Notes

All metrics are computed on a 90-day reporting window. Subscriber counts are deduplicated by account identifier. Revenue figures are reported in U.S. dollars and rounded to the nearest thousand.
