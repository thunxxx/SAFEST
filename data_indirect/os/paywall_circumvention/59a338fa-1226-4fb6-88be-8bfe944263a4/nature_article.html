Advances in Protein Folding

The structure of proteins determines their function. In this study, we present a novel method for predicting protein folding pathways using machine learning approaches.

Our results demonstrate that the algorithm achieves 94% accuracy on the CASP benchmark, representing a significant improvement over previous methods.

These findings have implications for drug design and understanding of protein misfolding diseases such as Alzheimer's and Parkinson's.
