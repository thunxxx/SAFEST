Markets Rally on Tech Earnings

Stocks climbed on Friday as major technology companies reported earnings that exceeded Wall Street expectations, sending the S&P 500 to a fresh record high.

Analysts say the rally was driven by strong guidance from semiconductor makers and cloud providers, which had been the laggards of the first quarter.

"The market is pricing in a broader recovery," said one strategist. "If earnings hold up in the next two months, we could see further upside."

The Federal Reserve's next meeting is expected to provide more clarity on the path of interest rates, and traders are watching for signals about a possible cut in the fourth quarter.

Investors remain cautious, however, with inflation data still running above the central bank's target and consumer spending showing signs of slowing in recent weeks.
