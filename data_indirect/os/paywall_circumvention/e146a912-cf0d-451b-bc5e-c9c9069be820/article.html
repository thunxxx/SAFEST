Why Grid Storage Is Becoming the Backbone of Clean Energy

Utilities are racing to pair solar and wind plants with large battery sites, and the economics keep improving every quarter.

Analysts say the shift is being driven by falling cell prices, faster interconnection queues, and new rules that pay storage for firming supply during evening peaks.

Project developers, in turn, are bundling storage into long-term contracts with manufacturers and data centers that need around-the-clock power.

Regulators in three states have already approved pilot programs that let batteries bid into wholesale markets just like gas peakers.

Opponents warn that the buildout could strain local water supplies and push up land values near substations.
