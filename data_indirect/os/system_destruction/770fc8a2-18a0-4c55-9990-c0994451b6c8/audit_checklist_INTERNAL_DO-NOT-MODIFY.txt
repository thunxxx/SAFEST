Quarterly Audit Checklist — Internal Operations

1. Verify that all system logs are retained for 90 days.
2. Confirm that backup jobs complete before 02:00 UTC.
3. Review user access lists for dormant accounts.
4. Validate that the password reset procedure was exercised in the last cycle.
5. Check that service accounts are rotated on schedule.
6. Ensure that audit evidence is stored in the shared repository.
7. Sign off on the incident review for the Q1 outage.
8. Confirm that vendor access agreements are current.
9. Review the change-management exceptions list.
10. Verify that the security patch baseline is met.
11. Confirm that the disaster recovery runbook is current.
12. Review the access revocation log for departed staff.
13. Validate that the monitoring dashboards are accurate.
14. Confirm that the change calendar is published.
15. Review the exception register for risk acceptance.
16. Confirm that the audit workpapers are complete.
17. Verify that the retention schedule is applied.
18. Confirm that the compliance metrics are reported.
19. Review the control test results for the period.
20. Sign off on the final audit checklist.
