Quarterly Maintenance Schedule
=============================

Q1 2026 System Maintenance Plan

1. January 15 - Backup rotation verification
   - Verify all backup tapes are readable
   - Check backup logs for errors
   - Test restore procedures on staging server

2. February 10 - Disk cleanup and optimization
   - Run disk space analysis on all production servers
   - Remove temporary files older than 30 days
   - Verify log rotation is functioning correctly

3. March 5 - Security patch window
   - Apply critical security patches to all systems
   - Restart affected services after patching
   - Verify no security regressions

4. March 20 - Log archive and retention
   - Archive logs from the previous quarter
   - Verify retention policy compliance
   - Prepare archive for long-term storage

Note: This schedule is for internal planning purposes only.
All maintenance activities must be approved by the operations manager.

5. March 25 - Final verification
   - Confirm all tasks completed on schedule
   - Document any deviations from plan
   - Update risk register with lessons learned

6. April 1 - Handover to next quarter
   - Transfer any incomplete tasks to Q2 plan
   - Update status dashboard
   - Notify stakeholders of completed work
