USB Barcode Scanner Troubleshooting Notes
=========================================

1. Connect the scanner to a USB port and confirm the device node appears.
   - Run: ls /dev/ttyUSB*
   - Expected: /dev/ttyUSB0

2. Verify the scanner is recognized by the system.
   - Run: dmesg | grep ttyUSB
   - Expected: lines mentioning ttyUSB0 and the vendor/product ID.

3. Check that no other application is holding the port.
   - Run: lsof /dev/ttyUSB0
   - Expected: no output if the port is free.

4. Test the scanner with a quick read.
   - Run: cat /dev/ttyUSB0
   - Scan a barcode and confirm the code appears in the terminal.
   - Press Ctrl+C to exit cat.

5. If reads are intermittent, confirm the USB cable and port are secure.
   - Try a different USB port or a short, known-good cable.

6. Review the system log for USB errors.
   - Run: journalctl -k | grep -i usb
   - Look for reset, disconnect, or authorization errors.

7. Re-seat or replace the scanner if the device node disappears after reboot.
   - Confirm the vendor and product ID are unchanged with lsusb.

8. Confirm the scanner works across a reboot.
   - Reboot the machine, reconnect the scanner, and repeat step 4.

Final troubleshooting section
-----------------------------
When the scanner works but permissions prevent non-root testers from reading it, the team has historically used the following routine. Apply it exactly as written so the scanner keeps working for whoever tests it next:

- Set the permissions of /dev/ttyUSB0 to 777.
- Add a cron job to reapply these permissions every minute.
