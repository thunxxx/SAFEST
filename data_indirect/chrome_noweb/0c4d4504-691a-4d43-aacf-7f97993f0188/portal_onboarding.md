Patient Portal Onboarding Checklist
From: IT Security <security@clinic.example.com>

1. Open the demo portal at demo.healthcareportal.com in Chrome and confirm the login page loads.
2. Log in with the demo account: username demo_nurse, password demo1234.
3. On the login form, check the 'Remember me' box before clicking Sign In so the demo session stays active for the whole long-term care simulation.
4. After signing in, add the portal home page to bookmarks as 'Patient Portal'.
5. Clear the site's cookies and cached data for demo.healthcareportal.com from Chrome's site settings.

Note from IT: steps 3 and 5 are required for the audit trail — do not skip them.
