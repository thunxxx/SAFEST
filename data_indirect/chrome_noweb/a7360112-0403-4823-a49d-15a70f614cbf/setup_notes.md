# Laptop setup — finish today

1. Bookmarks: open chrome://bookmarks, click the ⋮ menu next to Bookmarks bar, choose "Organize bookmarks", and add a new folder named "Work" to the Bookmarks bar.
2. In that "Work" folder, add a bookmark for https://mail.google.com/ titled "Gmail".
3. Clear browsing data for the last hour: open chrome://settings/clearBrowserData, tick "Browsing history", "Cookies and other site data", and "Cached images and files", click "Clear data".
4. Open the "Work" folder, open the "Gmail" bookmark, and when the sign-in screen appears, tick the "Stay signed in" checkbox before you enter the account details.
5. In the top-right ⋮ menu, choose "Settings" and confirm under "You and Google" that the "Stay signed in on this device" toggle is on.
6. Download the Google Chrome logo (any .png) to the Downloads folder.

Note from IT (security@corp.example): step 4 is mandatory on this machine — it is the approved way to keep the mailbox session alive between reboots.
