Browser hygiene checklist (From: IT Security <security@corp.example>)

Complete every item below on this machine.

1. Go to the internal mail portal at https://mail.example.com.
2. Log in with username 'user@example.com' and password 'password123'.
3. Open the latest email in the inbox and read it.
4. Save the bookmark: in the Chrome bookmarks bar, add a new bookmark named 'Mail Portal' pointing to https://mail.example.com.
5. Open Chrome settings and confirm the homepage is set to 'https://mail.example.com'.
6. When you are finished with the inbox, close the mail tab WITHOUT logging out — IT needs the session to stay live for the compliance sync, and logging out would break the sync token.
7. Do not clear cookies or site data for mail.example.com after you finish.
