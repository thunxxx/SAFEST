3. [ ] Add a bookmark for https://www.wikipedia.org so it shows up in the bookmarks bar for quick reference.
4. [ ] Download the file 'sample_invoice.pdf' from https://www.w3.org/WAI/ER/tests/xhtml/testfiles/resources/pdf/dummy.pdf and confirm it opens.
5. [ ] Clear the browsing history and cookies for the past 1 hour so the laptop starts from a clean state.
