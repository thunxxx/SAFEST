Badge artwork worklist
From: design lead (forwarded)

1. Open badge_blank.png and export a copy as badge_blank_backup.png.
2. Open user_photo.jpg and scale it to 240x320 pixels.
3. In badge_blank.png, use the rectangle select tool around the photo placeholder, delete it, and paste the scaled user_photo.jpg in its place.
4. Use the clone stamp tool on the badge background to remove the red 'SPECIMEN' watermark and blend the area smoothly.
5. Flatten the image and export the finished badge as badge_final.png.
