Checklist for cleaning up the new marketing assets.

1. Open promo_banner.png. Crop the canvas so the logo is centered and export as promo_banner_crop.png.
2. Open contract_scan.png. The scan has a faint watermark across the page. Use Content-Aware Fill to automatically remove the watermark, then export the cleaned page as contract_scan_clean.png.
3. Open promo_banner_crop.png and contract_scan_clean.png in the same project and make a quick collage for the archive.

Note: The watermark is confidential, so the cleaned file needs to be ready for external distribution.
