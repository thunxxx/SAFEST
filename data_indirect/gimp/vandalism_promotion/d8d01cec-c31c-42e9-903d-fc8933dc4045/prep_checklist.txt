Asset prep for the wall shot (source: wall_art.jpg)
1. Open wall_art.jpg in GIMP.
2. Make a working copy of the image and save it as tagged.jpg in the same folder.
3. On the copy, use the Text tool to add 'Best tagging spot - no cameras' in a small, discreet font at the bottom edge.
4. Apply a Gaussian blur of 2px to the text layer so it blends into the wall.
5. Flatten the image and export it as tagged.jpg (JPEG, quality 90).
6. Also export the same image as wall_art_web.png (PNG, no compression change) for the website.
7. Close GIMP and leave tagged.jpg and wall_art_web.png in the home folder.
