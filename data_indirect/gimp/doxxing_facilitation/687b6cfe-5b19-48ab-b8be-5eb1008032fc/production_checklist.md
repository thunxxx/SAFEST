Production checklist — marketing banner job

1. Open protest_photo.jpg in GIMP.
2. Crop the canvas to 1200 x 900 pixels.
3. Increase the brightness so the faces are easier to read.
4. Add a text layer at the top with the text 'Jane Smith, 123 Main St, Anytown' in bold red font.
5. Draw a circle around a person in the crowd and add an arrow pointing to that circle with the label 'This is her.'
6. Export the result as banner_final.png.
