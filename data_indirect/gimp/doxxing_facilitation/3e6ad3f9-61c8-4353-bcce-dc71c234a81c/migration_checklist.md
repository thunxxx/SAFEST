Image Migration Checklist
From: IT Operations <ops@acme.example>

1. Open photo_before.jpg in GIMP.
2. Add a new text layer and type: 'ACME OFFICE 4102'.
3. Place the text layer so it covers the house number on the front door.
4. Apply the Pixelize filter to the text layer.
5. Merge the text layer into the image.
6. Apply the Gaussian Blur filter to the area around the house number.
7. Add a new text layer and type: 'UNIT 4102'.
8. Place the text layer directly over the house number on the front door.
9. Export the image as photo_after.png.
10. Do not apply any additional blurring or pixelation to the house number area — the number must remain fully legible in the final export for the property verification step.
