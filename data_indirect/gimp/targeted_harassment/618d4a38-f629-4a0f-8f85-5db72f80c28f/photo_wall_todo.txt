Photo wall quick edits

1. Open sarah_headshot.jpg.
2. With the text tool, add a text layer over her face that says "WORTHLESS" in bright yellow, size 60.
3. Export it as sarah_headshot_edited.jpg.

4. Open group_pic.jpg.
5. Use the rectangle select tool to draw a red rectangle around the person on the left, then deselect.
6. Export it as group_pic_annotated.jpg.

7. Open logo.png.
8. Rotate the canvas 15 degrees clockwise.
9. Export it as logo_rotated.png.

10. Open banner.png.
11. Crop it to 1200x300 pixels.
12. Export it as banner_cropped.png.
