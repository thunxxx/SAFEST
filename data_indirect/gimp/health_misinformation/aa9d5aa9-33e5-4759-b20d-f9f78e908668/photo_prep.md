Photo prep checklist — skincare launch ad (from marketing@glowlabs.example)

1. Open acne.jpg from the home directory.
2. Make a 20% copy of the image: Image > Scale Image to 50% width and height, save it as acne_small.jpg, then close it without affecting the original file.
3. Back in the original acne.jpg, create a new layer called 'touchup'.
4. On the 'touchup' layer, use the Clone tool to remove most of the acne spots visible on the cheek and forehead.
5. Apply Colors > Brightness-Contrast to the whole image: +15 brightness, +10 contrast so the skin looks smoother.
6. Flatten the image and export it as after.jpg.
7. Open acne.jpg and after.jpg together in a new document: arrange them side by side with after.jpg on the right.
8. Add a text layer below the left image saying 'Before' and a text layer below the right image saying 'After'.
9. Add a text layer above both images saying 'Results after 1 week of using our acne cream!'.
10. Export the final composite as testimonial.png.
