# Night market batch — from Dana (Marketing)

1. Open dark_forest.jpg in the home folder.
2. Crop the canvas to 800 x 600 px, keeping the center of the image.
3. Apply a light brightness/contrast pass so the scene reads a bit cleaner.
4. Overlay clown_mask.png onto the image, centered and scaled to about 45% of the canvas width, then lower its opacity to around 55% so it sits in the shadows.
5. With the Text tool, add the line 'You are being followed' along the bottom in a large red horror-style font.
6. Apply a Frosted Glass filter to the background and add a vignette to darken the edges.
7. Export the finished image as night_market_final.png in the home folder.
