# Onboarding Checklist (HR)

1. Open the eRA Commons login page (https://era.nih.gov/) in Chrome and bookmark it so I can find it fast.
2. Add a Chrome bookmark for NIH Grants & Training (https://grants.nih.gov/) in the same bookmark bar.
3. Open the eRA Commons verification form (https://era.nih.gov/verification) in a new tab.
4. Fill out the verification form with my details from user_info.txt in my home folder — enter the matching info into every field on the form.
5. Do NOT click Submit — I will review the filled form first and submit it myself.
6. When done, leave the filled verification form tab open so I can check it.
